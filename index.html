Three ways to play
MOVE 01 · WORLD

Explore

Walk through towns and forests with your lead Pokémon beside you. Bring up the world map and fly back to any town you have visited.

MOVE 02 · BATTLE

Battle

Lock on, dodge in real time, pick your moves, and throw a Poke Ball while the battle plays out in the world around you. Gym Leaders put your team of six to the test.

MOVE 03 · TRAIN

Train

Every skill levels from 1 to 99 by doing it. Cast a line, swing a pick, cook over a campfire — the world keeps score.
