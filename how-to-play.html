Take the first step.

A little preparation, then a whole world to explore.

Get access to the game

The game runs on a private computer, so you join through Tailscale first. It is free.

  1. Ask the person who invited you to play for a Tailscale invitation and the game address.
  2. Install Tailscale on the device you will play on, from tailscale.com/download. Sign in with your own account, such as Google.
  3. Open the invite from your host and accept it. The game computer then shows up in your Tailscale.
  4. Make sure Tailscale says Connected. Then open the game address your host gives you in your browser. Keep Tailscale on while you play.

This website is separate from the game. Opening this page does not grant game access.

Choose your account

New players: enter a new trainer name to create your account. Returning players: enter your existing trainer name to load your saved progress. Use the same name each time. Only enter your own name, because anyone with access could type any trainer name.

Keep your progress

Slot 1 syncs to the game server. Other save slots stay in that browser. Use the menu's Save page before you leave.
