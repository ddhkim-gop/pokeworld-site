Take the first step.

A little preparation, then a whole world to explore.

Get access to the game

The game currently uses private Tailscale access. Accept the game's shared access and connect Tailscale before opening the game link your host sends you.

This website is separate from the game. Opening this page does not grant game access.

Choose your account

Open the game, choose your account name, and start playing. Use the same account next time to continue your progress.

Keep your progress

Slot 1 syncs to the game server. Other save slots stay in that browser. Use the menu's Save page before you leave.
